Full Catalog Every Marvel movie & series

Every title in the guide, grouped by the decade it came out. Filter by section or format, search the plots, or tick Connected only to hide the standalone titles.

All 143 titles in release order

  1. The Incredible Hulk (TV series) (1977)

    Series · CBS · 5 seasons, 82 episodes

    Bill Bixby plays David Banner and Lou Ferrigno is the Hulk. A drifter after a gamma experiment goes wrong, Banner moves from town to town with a reporter on his trail. It ran five seasons and spawned three TV films, one of which introduced Daredevil and Thor.

  2. The Amazing Spider-Man (TV series) (1977)

    Series · CBS · 2 seasons, 14 episodes

    Nicholas Hammond plays a grad-student Peter Parker in a low-budget live-action show. No Spidey villains appear; he fights ordinary crooks. Two seasons.

  3. Howard the Duck (1986)

    Film · Theatrical (Universal)

    Produced by George Lucas. A cigar-chomping duck is pulled from his planet to Cleveland, where he befriends a rock singer and fights a Dark Overlord. A famous flop, and the first theatrical Marvel film. Howard later cameos in Guardians of the Galaxy and Endgame.

  4. The Punisher (1989)

    Film · Home video (New World)

    Dolph Lundgren is Frank Castle, an ex-cop living in the sewers, who wages war on the mob after his family is killed. He ends up saving gangsters' kids from the Yakuza. Released straight to video in the US.

  5. Captain America (1990)

    Film · Home video (21st Century)

    Matt Salinger is Steve Rogers, frozen in 1944 after fighting an Italian Red Skull, then thawed in the 90s. Cheap, cheesy and only released on video, but it has the same basic plot as The First Avenger.

  6. X-Men: The Animated Series (1992)

    Animated · Fox Kids · 5 seasons, 76 episodes

    The classic cartoon with Wolverine, Storm, Rogue, Gambit and Jubilee. It adapted Days of Future Past and the Dark Phoenix Saga years before the films did. Xavier's yellow hover-chair and the theme tune both resurface in the MCU.

  7. Spider-Man: The Animated Series (1994)

    Animated · Fox Kids · 5 seasons, 65 episodes

    The 90s cartoon that ran alongside X-Men: TAS, with the alien costume, Carnage and a Secret Wars finale. Its multiverse ending, where Spider-Men from other worlds team up, predates the Spider-Verse films by 20 years.

  8. Generation X (1996)

    Film · Fox (TV film)

    Teen mutants Jubilee and Skin train at Emma Frost and Banshee's school and fight a mad scientist who enters dreams. A failed TV pilot.

  9. Blade (1998)

    Film · Theatrical (New Line)

    Wesley Snipes is the half-vampire "Daywalker", hunting vampires with his mentor Whistler. He stops Deacon Frost from becoming a blood god. Its success showed studios Marvel films could work, two years before X-Men. Snipes plays the role again in Deadpool & Wolverine.

  10. Nick Fury: Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D. (1998)

    Film · Fox (TV film)

    David Hasselhoff plays an eyepatched Fury who comes out of retirement to stop HYDRA, led by the Baron's children, from releasing a virus.

  11. X-Men (2000)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Rogue runs away after her powers put a boy in a coma. She and Logan are brought to Xavier's school. Magneto kidnaps Rogue to power a machine that would mutate world leaders at a summit on Ellis Island. The X-Men stop him, Senator Kelly dies, and Magneto ends up in a plastic prison.

  12. Blade II (2002)

    Film · Theatrical (New Line)

    Directed by Guillermo del Toro. Blade rescues Whistler and is forced to team up with the vampire elite to stop the Reapers, a mutated strain that feeds on vampires.

  13. Spider-Man (2002)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Tobey Maguire's Peter Parker is bitten by a genetically altered spider, learns that great power brings great responsibility after Uncle Ben's murder, and fights Norman Osborn's Green Goblin. He turns MJ down at the end to keep her safe.

  14. Daredevil (2003)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Ben Affleck's blind lawyer Matt Murdock fights crime in Hell's Kitchen. He falls for Elektra (Jennifer Garner), who is killed by Bullseye (Colin Farrell) on the orders of the Kingpin (Michael Clarke Duncan).

  15. X2 (2003)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Colonel William Stryker raids the school and uses a brainwashed Xavier and a copy of Cerebro to try to kill every mutant. The X-Men team up with Magneto, Nightcrawler is introduced, and Logan learns he was experimented on at Alkali Lake. Jean sacrifices herself to save the team as the dam floods.

  16. Hulk (2003)

    Film · Theatrical (Universal)

    Ang Lee's moody take with Eric Bana. Bruce's father David (Nick Nolte) experimented on him as a child, and later becomes a villain who can absorb any material. It has nothing to do with the MCU; The Incredible Hulk (2008) is a reboot.

  17. Spider-Man 2 (2004)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Peter's double life is falling apart and his powers start to fail. Otto Octavius's fusion experiment fuses mechanical arms to his spine, turning him into Doc Ock. Peter stops the train and saves Otto's soul, and MJ finds out his secret. Often called the best Spider-Man film.

  18. The Punisher (2004)

    Film · Theatrical (Lionsgate)

    Thomas Jane's FBI agent Frank Castle loses his whole family in a massacre ordered by a Tampa crime boss (John Travolta). He turns his enemies against each other.

  19. Blade: Trinity (2004)

    Film · Theatrical (New Line)

    Framed for murder, Blade joins the Nightstalkers (Ryan Reynolds and Jessica Biel) to fight a revived Dracula. It was the end of the Snipes trilogy.

  20. Elektra (2005)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Brought back from the dead, Elektra works as an assassin until she refuses a job and protects a father and daughter from the Hand. Jennifer Garner returns as Elektra in Deadpool & Wolverine.

  21. Fantastic Four (2005)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Reed, Sue, Johnny and Ben get their powers from a cosmic storm in space, and so does their sponsor Victor von Doom. Chris Evans plays Johnny Storm, and he comes back as Johnny in Deadpool & Wolverine.

  22. Man-Thing (2005)

    Film · Sci Fi Channel

    A swamp creature kills oil-company workers in the Louisiana bayou. A low-budget horror film that skipped cinemas in the US. The MCU's Man-Thing later appears in Werewolf by Night.

  23. X-Men: The Last Stand (2006)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Jean comes back as the Phoenix, kills Cyclops and later Xavier, and joins Magneto. A "cure" for mutation splits mutantkind. Magneto attacks Alcatraz, Logan is forced to kill Jean, and Magneto loses his powers. Kelsey Grammer plays Beast, a role he repeats in The Marvels.

  24. Blade: The Series (2006)

    Series · Spike · 13 episodes

    Sticky Fingaz takes over as Blade in Detroit, hunting the vampire House of Chthon. It continues on from the films. One season.

  25. Ghost Rider (2007)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Stunt rider Johnny Blaze (Nicolas Cage) sells his soul to Mephistopheles to save his dad and becomes the flaming-skulled Spirit of Vengeance. Sam Elliott plays the previous Rider.

  26. Spider-Man 3 (2007)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    An alien symbiote turns Peter arrogant ("emo Peter"). Harry takes up the Goblin mantle, Sandman turns out to be Ben's real killer, and Eddie Brock becomes Venom. Harry dies saving Peter. Sandman returns in No Way Home.

  27. Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer (2007)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    The Silver Surfer arrives to prepare Earth for Galactus, who is shown as a cosmic cloud. Doom steals the Surfer's board, and the Surfer turns on his master.

  28. Iron Man (2008)

    Film · Theatrical

    Arms dealer Tony Stark is kidnapped in Afghanistan, builds a suit to escape, and turns his company away from weapons. He defeats Obadiah Stane's Iron Monger and tells the world "I am Iron Man." Nick Fury's post-credits scene starts the Avengers Initiative.

  29. The Incredible Hulk (2008)

    Film · Theatrical

    Edward Norton's Bruce Banner hides in Brazil while General Ross hunts him. Soldier Emil Blonsky takes a super-soldier serum and becomes the Abomination. Samuel Sterns is splashed with Banner's blood, setting up the Leader, who finally appears in Brave New World.

  30. The Spectacular Spider-Man (2008)

    Animated · Kids' WB / Disney XD · 2 seasons, 26 episodes

    An animated series about Peter's high-school years, rated one of the best Spider-Man adaptations. Cancelled after two seasons.

  31. Punisher: War Zone (2008)

    Film · Theatrical (Lionsgate)

    Ray Stevenson's Frank Castle throws mobster Billy Russo into a glass crusher, creating Jigsaw, and accidentally kills an undercover FBI agent.

  32. Wolverine and the X-Men (2009)

    Animated · Nicktoons · 26 episodes

    An animated series. Xavier and Jean vanish, Logan leads a reformed team, and a future Xavier warns that a Sentinel apocalypse is coming. It was cancelled after one season.

  33. X-Men Origins: Wolverine (2009)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Logan and his brother Victor fight through every American war from 1845 on, then join Stryker's Team X. Logan quits, is betrayed, gets an adamantium skeleton, and has his memories wiped by an adamantium bullet. Ryan Reynolds plays a mouth-sewn version of Deadpool.

  34. Iron Man 2 (2010)

    Film · Theatrical

    Palladium from his arc reactor is poisoning Tony, and Whiplash attacks at the Monaco Grand Prix. Natasha Romanoff is introduced undercover, and Rhodey becomes War Machine. Tony creates a new element from his father's notes. Coulson finds Thor's hammer.

  35. The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes (2010)

    Animated · Disney XD · 2 seasons, 52 episodes

    A two-season animated Avengers series loved for adapting the comics faithfully: Kang, the Skrull invasion and Galactus.

  36. Thor (2011)

    Film · Theatrical

    Odin banishes an arrogant Thor to New Mexico without his hammer. Loki learns he is a Frost Giant and tries to seize Asgard. Thor proves himself worthy, destroys the Bifrost, and is cut off from Jane Foster. Hawkeye cameos, and Loki is shown controlling Selvig to reach the Tesseract.

  37. Captain America: The First Avenger (2011)

    Film · Theatrical

    Skinny Steve Rogers becomes a super-soldier in 1943. He rescues Bucky and the Howling Commandos and battles the Red Skull's HYDRA, which is powered by the Tesseract. Bucky falls from a train, and Steve crashes the Valkyrie into the Arctic. He wakes up 70 years later.

  38. X-Men: First Class (2011)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    In 1962, young Charles Xavier and Erik Lehnsherr recruit mutants to stop Sebastian Shaw, the Nazi who killed Erik's mother, from starting a nuclear war during the Cuban Missile Crisis. Erik kills Shaw, and a deflected bullet paralyses Charles. Erik becomes Magneto, and Raven leaves with him as Mystique.

  39. The Avengers (2012)

    Film · Theatrical

    Loki steals the Tesseract and opens a portal for a Chitauri army. Fury assembles Iron Man, Cap, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye. Coulson's death unites them, and Tony flies a nuke through the portal. A post-credits scene reveals Thanos was behind it all.

  40. The Amazing Spider-Man (2012)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    A reboot with Andrew Garfield, focused on Peter's missing parents. Curt Connors becomes the Lizard. Captain Stacy dies and makes Peter promise to keep Gwen out of it.

  41. Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance (2012)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    In Eastern Europe, Johnny Blaze protects a boy the Devil wants as a new vessel.

  42. Ultimate Spider-Man (2012)

    Animated · Disney XD · 4 seasons, 104 episodes

    Peter trains with a S.H.I.E.L.D. team of teen heroes. Four seasons, including a Spider-Verse arc.

  43. Iron Man 3 (2013)

    Film · Theatrical

    Tony has panic attacks after New York. "The Mandarin" turns out to be actor Trevor Slattery, fronting for Aldrich Killian's Extremis soldiers. Tony blows up all his suits and has the shrapnel removed from his chest.

  44. Thor: The Dark World (2013)

    Film · Theatrical

    Malekith's Dark Elves want the Aether, later revealed as the Reality Stone, which is now inside Jane. Frigga is killed. Thor and Loki team up, Loki fakes his death and takes Odin's throne, and the Collector receives the Aether.

  45. The Wolverine (2013)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    A guilt-ridden Logan, haunted by Jean, goes to Japan to say goodbye to a man he saved at Nagasaki. He loses his healing factor for a while and fights the Silver Samurai. Two years later, Xavier and Magneto warn him of a new threat.

  46. Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. (2013)

    Series · ABC · 7 seasons, 136 episodes

    Coulson, revived by Kree blood, leads a team with Daisy (Quake), May, Fitz and Simmons. They survive HYDRA's uprising, Inhumans, Ghost Rider (Robbie Reyes), the Framework and time travel. Seven seasons; after the first two it mostly stops tying in with the films.

  47. Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)

    Film · Theatrical

    Steve finds HYDRA has secretly grown inside S.H.I.E.L.D., and its Winter Soldier is a brainwashed Bucky. Steve, Natasha and new partner Sam Wilson (Falcon) bring down the Helicarriers and S.H.I.E.L.D. with them. Bucky pulls Steve from the river.

  48. Guardians of the Galaxy (2014)

    Film · Theatrical

    Peter Quill steals an orb that holds the Power Stone. He teams with Gamora, Drax, Rocket and Groot to stop Ronan from destroying Xandar. Groot sacrifices himself and is regrown as a sapling, and the Nova Corps guard the stone.

  49. X-Men: Days of Future Past (2014)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    In 2023, Sentinels built from Mystique's shape-shifting DNA have nearly wiped out mutants. Kitty sends Logan's mind back to 1973 to stop Mystique from assassinating Bolivar Trask. Young Charles and Erik help, and Raven spares Trask at the White House. History rewrites itself, and Logan wakes up in a peaceful 2023 with Jean and Scott alive.

  50. The Amazing Spider-Man 2 (2014)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Electro, a dying Harry Osborn as Green Goblin, and Rhino. Gwen Stacy dies when Peter's web fails to catch her in time. Plans for a Sinister Six film were scrapped. In No Way Home, Garfield's Peter saves MJ as he couldn't save Gwen.

  51. Big Hero 6 (2014)

    Animated · Theatrical (Walt Disney Animation)

    A loosely adapted Marvel team. Robotics prodigy Hiro and the inflatable healthcare robot Baymax form a hero team in San Fransokyo. It won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature and led to a TV series.

  52. Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015)

    Film · Theatrical

    Tony and Bruce build Ultron from the Mind Stone, and it decides humans must go extinct. Wanda and Pietro join the team, Pietro dies, and Vision is born. Sokovia is lifted into the sky and destroyed, setting up the Sokovia Accords. Hulk leaves Earth.

  53. Ant-Man (2015)

    Film · Theatrical

    Ex-con Scott Lang is recruited by Hank Pym to steal the Yellowjacket suit. He shrinks into the Quantum Realm and gets out, which is the key to Endgame's time heist.

  54. Agent Carter (2015)

    Series · ABC · 2 seasons, 18 episodes

    In 1946, Peggy Carter works at the sexist SSR while secretly helping Howard Stark clear his name, with Jarvis at her side. Season 2 moves to Los Angeles and Darkforce.

  55. Daredevil (2015)

    Series · Netflix · 3 seasons, 39 episodes

    Blind lawyer Matt Murdock (Charlie Cox) fights Wilson Fisk (Vincent D'Onofrio) in Hell's Kitchen. The show is known for its single-take hallway fights. Season 2 brings in the Punisher and Elektra, and Season 3 has Fisk's return and Bullseye's origin. It was cancelled in 2018 and continued in Born Again.

  56. Jessica Jones (2015)

    Series · Netflix · 3 seasons, 39 episodes

    Hard-drinking PI Jessica Jones (Krysten Ritter) is hunted by Kilgrave (David Tennant), who once controlled her mind. Three seasons. Luke Cage debuts here.

  57. Fantastic Four (2015)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Josh Trank's dark reboot with Miles Teller. Teens teleport to Planet Zero and come back changed, then fight Doom. It was troubled behind the scenes and flopped.

  58. Captain America: Civil War (2016)

    Film · Theatrical

    The Sokovia Accords would put the Avengers under UN control. Tony signs and Steve refuses. Zemo frames Bucky to break the team apart, then shows Tony footage of Bucky killing his parents. Spider-Man and Black Panther make their debuts at the airport battle.

  59. Doctor Strange (2016)

    Film · Theatrical

    Surgeon Stephen Strange wrecks his hands in a car crash and learns magic in Kamar-Taj. He uses the Time Stone to trap Dormammu in a time loop. Mordo turns against the sorcerers.

  60. Deadpool (2016)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Wade Wilson, dying of cancer, is tortured into a disfigured, unkillable mercenary by Ajax. With Colossus and Negasonic Teenage Warhead, he rescues his girlfriend Vanessa. Fourth-wall jokes and an R rating made it a huge hit.

  61. X-Men: Apocalypse (2016)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    En Sabah Nur, the first mutant, wakes up in 1983 and recruits Magneto, Storm, Psylocke and Angel. Young Jean, Scott and Nightcrawler join the school, and Jean's Phoenix power destroys Apocalypse.

  62. Luke Cage (2016)

    Series · Netflix · 2 seasons, 26 episodes

    Bulletproof ex-con Luke Cage protects Harlem from Cottonmouth, Black Mariah and Diamondback. Season 2 ends with Luke running Harlem's Paradise.

  63. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017)

    Film · Theatrical

    Peter meets his father, Ego, a living planet that wants to absorb everything. Yondu dies saving Peter, and Gamora and Nebula start to make peace.

  64. Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017)

    Film · Theatrical

    After Civil War, 15-year-old Peter Parker desperately wants to be an Avenger. He takes on Adrian Toomes, the Vulture, a salvager selling Chitauri weapons who turns out to be his date Liz's father. Peter turns down Tony's offer to join the team.

  65. Thor: Ragnarok (2017)

    Film · Theatrical

    Odin dies and Hela, his firstborn, conquers Asgard. Thor is stranded on Sakaar, where Hulk is a gladiator. Thor loses an eye and Mjolnir, and Asgard is destroyed in Ragnarok. The refugees' ship meets Thanos's ship.

  66. Logan (2017)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    In 2029 almost no mutants are left. An ageing Logan, his healing failing, cares for a senile Xavier. He protects Laura (X-23), a girl cloned from his DNA, from Transigen. Xavier is killed by a Wolverine clone, and Logan dies saving the children. Deadpool & Wolverine later calls him the anchor being of his universe.

  67. Legion (2017)

    Series · FX · 3 seasons, 27 episodes

    David Haller, a powerful mutant diagnosed with schizophrenia, finds that a parasitic mind, the Shadow King, has lived in his head since childhood. He turns out to be Xavier's son. A surreal three-season show, separate from the films.

  68. The Gifted (2017)

    Series · Fox · 2 seasons, 29 episodes

    A suburban family goes on the run after their kids' powers emerge, in a world where the X-Men have vanished. Two seasons, separate from the films.

  69. Iron Fist (2017)

    Series · Netflix · 2 seasons, 23 episodes

    Billionaire heir Danny Rand returns from K'un-Lun with the Iron Fist and fights the Hand inside his own company. It was poorly received.

  70. The Defenders (2017)

    Series · Netflix · 8 episodes

    Daredevil, Jessica Jones, Luke Cage and Iron Fist team up against the Hand, led by Sigourney Weaver's Alexandra and a resurrected Elektra. Matt is buried under a collapsing building. Eight episodes.

  71. The Punisher (2017)

    Series · Netflix · 2 seasons, 26 episodes

    Jon Bernthal's Frank Castle uncovers the military conspiracy behind his family's murder and faces his former best friend, Billy Russo (Jigsaw). Two seasons. Bernthal returns in Born Again, One Last Kill and Brand New Day.

  72. Inhumans (2017)

    Series · ABC · 8 episodes

    Attilan's royal family is stranded in Hawaii after a coup by Maximus. Panned and cancelled after one season. Black Bolt later appears in Multiverse of Madness, played by the same actor.

  73. Runaways (2017)

    Series · Hulu · 3 seasons, 33 episodes

    Six LA teens find out their parents are a cult called the Pride, which serves an alien. Three seasons.

  74. Black Panther (2018)

    Film · Theatrical

    T'Challa becomes king, then is overthrown by his cousin Killmonger, who wants to arm oppressed people around the world. T'Challa wins back the throne and opens Wakanda up to the world.

  75. Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

    Film · Theatrical

    Thanos collects all six Infinity Stones. Loki, Gamora, Heimdall and Vision die. Thor's axe strikes Thanos but not his head, and he snaps, wiping out half of all life. Spider-Man, Black Panther and most of the Guardians turn to dust.

  76. Ant-Man and the Wasp (2018)

    Film · Theatrical

    Scott, under house arrest, helps Hope and Hank rescue Janet from the Quantum Realm. In the post-credits scene the snap hits, leaving Scott trapped in the Quantum Realm, which later lets him escape into Endgame.

  77. Deadpool 2 (2018)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    Vanessa is killed, and Wade tries to protect a young mutant, Russell, from Cable, a soldier from the future. He forms X-Force, most of whom die in the first five minutes, and uses Cable's time device to fix everyone's deaths in the credits.

  78. Cloak & Dagger (2018)

    Series · Freeform · 2 seasons, 20 episodes

    Two New Orleans teens get linked light and darkness powers on the night of an oil-rig explosion. Two seasons.

  79. Venom (2018)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Journalist Eddie Brock (Tom Hardy) bonds with the symbiote Venom, and they fight Riot and the Life Foundation. The mid-credits scene introduces Woody Harrelson's Cletus Kasady.

  80. Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse (2018)

    Animated · Theatrical (Sony)

    Brooklyn teen Miles Morales is bitten just as Kingpin's collider pulls in Spider-People from other worlds: Peter B. Parker, Gwen, Noir, Peni and Spider-Ham. Miles takes his leap of faith. It won the Oscar for Best Animated Feature.

  81. Captain Marvel (2019)

    Film · Theatrical

    In 1995, Kree soldier Vers learns she is really US pilot Carol Danvers. The Kree lied to her, and the Skrulls are refugees. A young Nick Fury loses his eye to Goose the Flerken. Fury names the Avengers Initiative after her call sign.

  82. Avengers: Endgame (2019)

    Film · Theatrical

    Five years after the snap, Scott escapes the Quantum Realm, and the Avengers time-travel to collect the stones. Natasha sacrifices herself on Vormir, Hulk snaps everyone back, and Tony dies snapping Thanos away. Steve returns the stones, lives a life with Peggy, and hands Sam the shield. A 2012 Loki escapes with the Tesseract.

  83. Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019)

    Film · Theatrical

    On a school trip to Europe, Peter inherits Tony's EDITH glasses and is tricked by Mysterio, an illusionist. Mysterio dies, but a doctored video frames Peter, and J. Jonah Jameson reveals that Peter Parker is Spider-Man.

  84. Dark Phoenix (2019)

    Film · Theatrical (Fox)

    In 1992, Jean absorbs a cosmic force during a space rescue, loses control, and kills Mystique. Shape-shifting D'Bari aliens want the power. Jean sacrifices herself and becomes the Phoenix. It was the last Fox X-Men team film.

  85. The New Mutants (2020)

    Film · Theatrical (20th Century)

    Five teen mutants are held in a hospital run by the Essex Corporation. Dani Moonstar's powers bring their worst fears to life, including the Demon Bear. Its links to the films are only loose.

  86. Helstrom (2020)

    Series · Hulu · 10 episodes

    The son and daughter of a serial killer hunt demons. It was cancelled after one season.

  87. WandaVision (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    A grieving Wanda traps the town of Westview inside sitcom realities with a recreated Vision and twin sons. Agatha Harkness reveals Wanda is the Scarlet Witch. Wanda lets the town go, losing Vision and the boys, and starts reading the Darkhold. S.W.O.R.D. rebuilds a White Vision, who flies off.

  88. The Falcon and the Winter Soldier (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Sam gives up the shield, and the government hands it to John Walker, who murders a Flag Smasher in public. Bucky makes amends and Zemo escapes. Sam takes up the shield as the new Captain America, and Valentina recruits Walker.

  89. Loki (Season 1) (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    The 2012 Loki is arrested by the TVA, which prunes every branch from the Sacred Timeline. He meets variants of himself, including Sylvie, and reaches the Citadel at the End of Time. Sylvie kills He Who Remains, and the multiverse branches out of control.

  90. What If...? (Season 1) (2021)

    Animated · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    The Watcher shows alternate MCU stories: Peggy becomes Captain Carter, T'Challa becomes Star-Lord, Strange loses his heart and his world, and the Avengers fall to zombies. It ends with a team of heroes stopping an Infinity Ultron.

  91. Black Widow (2021)

    Film · Theatrical / Disney+

    Set after Civil War. Natasha reunites with her fake Russian family: sister Yelena, Red Guardian and Melina. She destroys the Red Room and frees the brainwashed Widows. In 2023, Yelena is told Clint is to blame for Natasha's death.

  92. Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings (2021)

    Film · Theatrical

    Shaun, a San Francisco valet, is really Shang-Chi, son of Wenwu, who has led the Ten Rings for 1,000 years. Wenwu is lured by a voice that sounds like his dead wife and dies saving his son from a soul-eating creature. The Ten Rings respond to Wong. Simu Liu is in the announced Doomsday cast.

  93. Eternals (2021)

    Film · Theatrical

    Ten immortals sent by the Celestial Arishem learn that Earth is an egg for a new Celestial, Tiamut, whose birth would destroy all life. They stop it, freezing Tiamut in the Indian Ocean. Introduces Harry Styles as Eros.

  94. Hawkeye (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    At Christmas, Clint teams up with Kate Bishop to deal with his Ronin past. Yelena comes for revenge. Vincent D'Onofrio's Kingpin returns from the Netflix shows as the head of the Tracksuit Mafia, and Echo shoots him.

  95. Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

    Film · Theatrical

    With his identity public, Peter asks Strange for a spell, and Peter's meddling breaks it. Villains from other universes arrive: Green Goblin, Doc Ock, Electro, Sandman and Lizard. Aunt May dies. Tobey Maguire's and Andrew Garfield's Spider-Men help cure them. Strange erases Peter Parker from everyone's memory, and Charlie Cox's Matt Murdock appears.

  96. Spidey and His Amazing Friends (2021)

    Animated · Disney Junior · 4 seasons, 64 episodes

    A preschool show with Peter, Miles and Gwen as a junior Spider-team.

  97. Venom: Let There Be Carnage (2021)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Cletus Kasady becomes Carnage and breaks out his lover, Shriek. In the mid-credits scene, Eddie and Venom are pulled into the MCU, where Peter Parker is on TV, and Venom leaves a piece of the symbiote behind.

  98. M.O.D.O.K. (2021)

    Animated · Hulu · 10 episodes

    A stop-motion comedy about the giant-headed villain's midlife crisis, voiced by Patton Oswalt.

  99. Hit-Monkey (2021)

    Animated · Hulu · 2 seasons, 20 episodes

    A Japanese snow monkey becomes an assassin, guided by the ghost of a hitman. Two seasons.

  100. Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022)

    Film · Theatrical

    America Chavez can travel between universes, and Wanda, corrupted by the Darkhold, hunts her to reach her sons in another world. On Earth-838 the Illuminati are slaughtered: Patrick Stewart's Xavier, John Krasinski's Reed Richards, Captain Carter, Black Bolt and Captain Marvel (Maria Rambeau). Wanda destroys the Darkhold and brings Mount Wundagore down on herself. Strange gets a third eye.

  101. Moon Knight (2022)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Gift-shop worker Steven Grant learns he shares a body with mercenary Marc Spector, avatar of the Egyptian moon god Khonshu. They stop Arthur Harrow from freeing Ammit. A third identity, Jake Lockley, is revealed.

  102. Ms. Marvel (2022)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Jersey teen Kamala Khan's bangle unlocks light powers tied to the Noor dimension and her family's history in Partition-era India. Bruno says she has a genetic "mutation", and the X-Men '97 theme plays. In the post-credits scene she swaps places with Captain Marvel.

  103. Thor: Love and Thunder (2022)

    Film · Theatrical

    Gorr the God Butcher wants to kill every god. Jane Foster, dying of cancer, wields a rebuilt Mjolnir as the Mighty Thor and dies in battle. Thor adopts Gorr's daughter, Love.

  104. She-Hulk: Attorney at Law (2022)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Lawyer Jennifer Walters gets Hulk powers from Bruce's blood and runs a superhuman law division. Abomination is paroled, and Bruce returns with his son Skaar. Charlie Cox's Daredevil shows up as both a rival lawyer and a love interest. The finale breaks the fourth wall.

  105. Werewolf by Night (2022)

    Special · Disney+

    A black-and-white horror special. Monster hunters compete for the Bloodstone, and one of them, Jack Russell, turns out to be a werewolf. Man-Thing is his friend. It isn't tied to any other story.

  106. Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022)

    Film · Theatrical

    Wakanda mourns T'Challa. Namor's undersea Talokan threatens war over vibranium, and young inventor Riri Williams is caught in the middle. Ramonda dies, and Shuri becomes Black Panther. T'Challa's son is revealed in Haiti.

  107. I Am Groot (2022)

    Animated · Disney+ · 2 seasons, 10 episodes

    Short animated pieces about Baby Groot's mischief on the Guardians' ship. Two seasons (2022, 2023).

  108. The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special (2022)

    Special · Disney+

    Drax and Mantis kidnap Kevin Bacon as a Christmas present for Peter. It reveals that Mantis is Peter's half-sister, and the team now owns Knowhere.

  109. Morbius (2022)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Dr Michael Morbius cures his blood disease with vampire-bat DNA and becomes a living vampire. In the mid-credits scene, Michael Keaton's Vulture has been sent to this world by No Way Home's spell.

  110. Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania (2023)

    Film · Theatrical

    Cassie's signal pulls the family into the Quantum Realm, where Janet's exiled ally Kang the Conqueror rules. Kang is seemingly defeated, but a council of his variants gathers. Marvel later dropped the Kang story, and Doctor Doom became the saga's villain instead.

  111. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023)

    Film · Theatrical

    Rocket is badly wounded, and the team raids the High Evolutionary, the scientist who mutilated him. Rocket's past is shown and he becomes captain. The team goes separate ways, and Peter goes home to his grandfather on Earth.

  112. Secret Invasion (2023)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Rogue Skrulls led by Gravik have replaced world leaders and try to spark a world war. Maria Hill and Talos die, Rhodey is revealed to have been a Skrull for years, and G'iah gains the powers of every Avenger.

  113. Loki (Season 2) (2023)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    The TVA's loom is failing under the branching timelines. Loki learns to time-slip, meets Victor Timely and keeps reliving the collapse. He finally takes the throne at the End of Time and holds the branches together as a living Yggdrasil. Tom Hiddleston is in the announced Doomsday cast.

  114. What If...? (Season 2) (2023)

    Animated · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Nine more alternate tales, including Nebula as a noir detective, 80s Avengers, and the introduction of Kahhori. Captain Carter returns, and the Watcher's ally Strange Supreme imprisons rogue variants.

  115. The Marvels (2023)

    Film · Theatrical

    Carol, Monica Rambeau and Kamala swap places whenever they use their powers. Kree leader Dar-Benn tears holes in space. Monica is trapped in another universe where her mother is Binary, and she meets Kelsey Grammer's Beast. Kamala starts recruiting Kate Bishop for a Young Avengers team.

  116. Moon Girl and Devil Dinosaur (2023)

    Animated · Disney Channel · 2 seasons, 30 episodes

    Thirteen-year-old genius Lunella Lafayette and a time-displaced T. rex protect the Lower East Side. Two seasons.

  117. Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse (2023)

    Animated · Theatrical (Sony)

    Miles follows Gwen to Miguel O'Hara's Spider-Society and learns about "canon events": his dad's death is supposed to happen. He refuses and ends up on a world where he became the Prowler. Donald Glover appears in live action as the Prowler, the role he set up in Homecoming. It ends on a cliffhanger.

  118. Echo (2024)

    Series · Disney+ / Hulu · 5 episodes

    After shooting Kingpin in Hawkeye, Maya Lopez goes home to Oklahoma and connects with her Choctaw ancestors' powers. Kingpin survives and comes after her. She uses her powers to heal his trauma, and Fisk heads back to New York to run for mayor. It opens with a Daredevil fight.

  119. X-Men '97 (2024)

    Animated · Disney+ · 10 episodes

    Picks up after the 1992 series. With Xavier gone, Magneto leads the X-Men. Genosha is wiped out by Sentinels, and Bastion's Operation: Zero Tolerance hunts mutants. Magneto rips the metal out of the Earth and Wolverine loses his adamantium. The team ends up scattered across time.

  120. Deadpool & Wolverine (2024)

    Film · Theatrical

    The TVA plans to let Deadpool's world (Earth-10005) die slowly, because its anchor being, Logan, died. Wade grabs a "worst" Wolverine variant. They're dumped in the Void, ruled by Cassandra Nova, where they find Blade, Elektra, X-23, Gambit and Johnny Storm. They save the timeline, and this Logan stays on Earth-10005.

  121. Agatha All Along (2024)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Freed from Wanda's spell, Agatha leads a coven down the Witches' Road. Teen is revealed as Billy Maximoff reborn, and Rio Vidal is Death herself. Agatha dies and returns as a ghost guiding Billy, who sets off to find his brother Tommy.

  122. What If...? (Season 3) (2024)

    Animated · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    The final season: Hulk vs. kaiju, the Red Guardian and Winter Soldier in the 80s, and a war in which the Watcher himself is hunted.

  123. Venom: The Last Dance (2024)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Back in his own world, Eddie and Venom are hunted by Knull's creatures, who want the codex. Venom sacrifices himself, and Tom Hardy's run ends.

  124. Madame Web (2024)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Set in 2003. Paramedic Cassie Webb sees the future and protects three girls destined to be Spider-Women from Ezekiel Sims. Panned, and it connects to nothing.

  125. Kraven the Hunter (2024)

    Film · Theatrical (Sony)

    Sergei Kravinoff (Aaron Taylor-Johnson) gets lion powers and hunts criminals, then turns on his crime-boss father. It was the last film in Sony's universe.

  126. Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man (2025)

    Animated · Disney+ · 10 episodes

    An alternate-universe Peter Parker's first year, in a comic-book art style. Norman Osborn becomes his mentor instead of Tony Stark, and Doc Ock is introduced. A second season is coming in late 2026.

  127. Captain America: Brave New World (2025)

    Film · Theatrical

    Thaddeus Ross is now President, and adamantium has been found on Celestial Island. Samuel Sterns, the Leader, manipulates events through mind control. Sam takes on the role with Joaquin Torres as his Falcon, and Ross turns into the Red Hulk at the White House. Sam starts reassembling the Avengers.

  128. Daredevil: Born Again (2025)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Bullseye kills Foggy, and Matt Murdock hangs up the suit. Wilson Fisk is elected mayor of New York and creates an anti-vigilante task force. Frank Castle is back, the serial killer Muse is stopped, and Fisk declares martial law. Matt builds an army to take the city back.

  129. Thunderbolts* (2025)

    Film · Theatrical

    Valentina tries to wipe out her own expendable agents, and Yelena, Ghost, John Walker and Taskmaster survive. With Bucky and Red Guardian they face Bob, a super-soldier who is also the Void, a dark force that swallows New York. Yelena reaches Bob, and Valentina announces them as the "New Avengers". The team is in the Doomsday cast.

  130. Ironheart (2025)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Kicked out of MIT, Riri Williams goes back to Chicago and joins the Hood's crew to fund her armour. Her AI is a recreation of her dead friend Natalie. Hood's powers come from a demonic cloak, and the season ends with Riri making a deal with Mephisto.

  131. The Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025)

    Film · Theatrical

    On the retro-futurist Earth-828 the Fantastic Four are the world's only heroes. The Silver Surfer heralds Galactus, who wants Sue's unborn son Franklin. Sue gives birth, and the team moves the whole planet's population to beat Galactus. Franklin restores Sue's life. In the post-credits scene, Doom visits Franklin.

  132. Eyes of Wakanda (2025)

    Animated · Disney+ · 4 episodes

    Four animated episodes across history about Wakanda's Hatut Zeraze war dogs, who recover stolen vibranium. The story runs from the Bronze Age to the Trojan War, and one episode features an early Iron Fist.

  133. Marvel Zombies (2025)

    Animated · Disney+ · 4 episodes

    An adult-rated animated miniseries. In the What If zombie universe, Kamala, Kate Bishop, Yelena and others fight a Queen of the Dead version of Wanda.

  134. Wonder Man (2026)

    Series · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    Struggling actor Simon Williams, who secretly has powers, auditions for a reboot of the film Wonder Man while hiding his abilities from Hollywood's anti-super rules. Ben Kingsley's Trevor Slattery returns as his acting partner. It was released in January 2026.

  135. Daredevil: Born Again (Season 2) (2026)

    Series · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    Matt's resistance takes on Mayor Fisk's regime. In the finale, Karen Page is on trial and Matt cross-examines Fisk, then publicly reveals he is Daredevil. Karen is cleared and Fisk is pushed into a deal, but Matt is arrested. Jessica Jones returns and ends the season on a cliffhanger.

  136. The Punisher: One Last Kill (2026)

    Special · Disney+

    A roughly 45-minute, mature-rated Marvel Television Special co-written by Jon Bernthal and director Reinaldo Marcus Green. It premiered 12 May 2026, the same week as Born Again Season 2's finale, and carries Frank Castle into Spider-Man: Brand New Day.

  137. Spider-Noir (2026)

    Series · MGM+ / Prime Video · 8 episodes

    Nicolas Cage is Ben Reilly, a washed-up private eye in 1930s New York who was once the city's only superhero. You can watch it in black and white or in colour. It premiered 27 May 2026 to strong reviews, with Lamorne Morris and Brendan Gleeson co-starring. Cage voiced Spider-Noir in Into the Spider-Verse.

  138. X-Men '97 (Season 2) (2026)

    Animated · Disney+

    After Asteroid M, the team is lost in time: Xavier, Magneto, Rogue and Nightcrawler in ancient Egypt, where Apocalypse is rising, and Cyclops and Jean in the far future. It premiered 1 July 2026, and a third season is confirmed.

  139. Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026)

    Film · Theatrical

    Nobody remembers Peter Parker, so he is Spider-Man full time, but alone. His powers start changing in ways he can't control. A body-jumping psychic threat targets Damage Control, and it's tied to Sadie Sink's Jean Grey searching for her sister. Damage Control's Bill Metzger is the real villain. Scorpion, Tombstone and the Punisher appear. It opened 31 July 2026 with a record-breaking weekend.

  140. VisionQuest (2026)

    Series · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    The final part of the WandaVision trilogy. The rebuilt White Vision tries to recover his memories and his humanity. James Spader's Ultron, Faran Tahir's Raza (from Iron Man) and a human E.D.I.T.H. appear. It premiered 14 October 2026.

  141. Avengers: Doomsday (2026)

    Film · Theatrical

    In cinemas 18 December 2026. Robert Downey Jr. plays Doctor Doom, and the trailer shows an army of Sentinels. The confirmed cast brings together MCU Avengers (including Chris Evans' Steve Rogers), Wakandans, the Thunderbolts and the Fantastic Four, plus Fox X-Men veterans: Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, Alan Cumming, Kelsey Grammer and Channing Tatum's Gambit.

  142. Avengers: Secret Wars (2027)

    Film · Theatrical

    Planned for December 2027 as the end of the Multiverse Saga. Sadie Sink's Jean Grey is confirmed to return. Plot details are not public yet.

  143. Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse (2027)

    Animated · Theatrical (Sony)

    In cinemas 18 June 2027. Concludes Miles's story after Across the Spider-Verse's cliffhanger.
