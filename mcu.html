MCU Timeline Watch order, story & release

Story order follows the story from 1943 onward, the way you would watch it to keep up. Optional extras like What If...? sit under the title they follow. Release order groups everything by phase. Open only the sections you want. Spoilers throughout.

All 66 titles in release order

  1. Iron Man (2008)

    Film · Theatrical

    Arms dealer Tony Stark is kidnapped in Afghanistan, builds a suit to escape, and turns his company away from weapons. He defeats Obadiah Stane's Iron Monger and tells the world "I am Iron Man." Nick Fury's post-credits scene starts the Avengers Initiative.

  2. The Incredible Hulk (2008)

    Film · Theatrical

    Edward Norton's Bruce Banner hides in Brazil while General Ross hunts him. Soldier Emil Blonsky takes a super-soldier serum and becomes the Abomination. Samuel Sterns is splashed with Banner's blood, setting up the Leader, who finally appears in Brave New World.

  3. Iron Man 2 (2010)

    Film · Theatrical

    Palladium from his arc reactor is poisoning Tony, and Whiplash attacks at the Monaco Grand Prix. Natasha Romanoff is introduced undercover, and Rhodey becomes War Machine. Tony creates a new element from his father's notes. Coulson finds Thor's hammer.

  4. Thor (2011)

    Film · Theatrical

    Odin banishes an arrogant Thor to New Mexico without his hammer. Loki learns he is a Frost Giant and tries to seize Asgard. Thor proves himself worthy, destroys the Bifrost, and is cut off from Jane Foster. Hawkeye cameos, and Loki is shown controlling Selvig to reach the Tesseract.

  5. Captain America: The First Avenger (2011)

    Film · Theatrical

    Skinny Steve Rogers becomes a super-soldier in 1943. He rescues Bucky and the Howling Commandos and battles the Red Skull's HYDRA, which is powered by the Tesseract. Bucky falls from a train, and Steve crashes the Valkyrie into the Arctic. He wakes up 70 years later.

  6. The Avengers (2012)

    Film · Theatrical

    Loki steals the Tesseract and opens a portal for a Chitauri army. Fury assembles Iron Man, Cap, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye. Coulson's death unites them, and Tony flies a nuke through the portal. A post-credits scene reveals Thanos was behind it all.

  7. Iron Man 3 (2013)

    Film · Theatrical

    Tony has panic attacks after New York. "The Mandarin" turns out to be actor Trevor Slattery, fronting for Aldrich Killian's Extremis soldiers. Tony blows up all his suits and has the shrapnel removed from his chest.

  8. Thor: The Dark World (2013)

    Film · Theatrical

    Malekith's Dark Elves want the Aether, later revealed as the Reality Stone, which is now inside Jane. Frigga is killed. Thor and Loki team up, Loki fakes his death and takes Odin's throne, and the Collector receives the Aether.

  9. Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014)

    Film · Theatrical

    Steve finds HYDRA has secretly grown inside S.H.I.E.L.D., and its Winter Soldier is a brainwashed Bucky. Steve, Natasha and new partner Sam Wilson (Falcon) bring down the Helicarriers and S.H.I.E.L.D. with them. Bucky pulls Steve from the river.

  10. Guardians of the Galaxy (2014)

    Film · Theatrical

    Peter Quill steals an orb that holds the Power Stone. He teams with Gamora, Drax, Rocket and Groot to stop Ronan from destroying Xandar. Groot sacrifices himself and is regrown as a sapling, and the Nova Corps guard the stone.

  11. Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015)

    Film · Theatrical

    Tony and Bruce build Ultron from the Mind Stone, and it decides humans must go extinct. Wanda and Pietro join the team, Pietro dies, and Vision is born. Sokovia is lifted into the sky and destroyed, setting up the Sokovia Accords. Hulk leaves Earth.

  12. Ant-Man (2015)

    Film · Theatrical

    Ex-con Scott Lang is recruited by Hank Pym to steal the Yellowjacket suit. He shrinks into the Quantum Realm and gets out, which is the key to Endgame's time heist.

  13. Captain America: Civil War (2016)

    Film · Theatrical

    The Sokovia Accords would put the Avengers under UN control. Tony signs and Steve refuses. Zemo frames Bucky to break the team apart, then shows Tony footage of Bucky killing his parents. Spider-Man and Black Panther make their debuts at the airport battle.

  14. Doctor Strange (2016)

    Film · Theatrical

    Surgeon Stephen Strange wrecks his hands in a car crash and learns magic in Kamar-Taj. He uses the Time Stone to trap Dormammu in a time loop. Mordo turns against the sorcerers.

  15. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017)

    Film · Theatrical

    Peter meets his father, Ego, a living planet that wants to absorb everything. Yondu dies saving Peter, and Gamora and Nebula start to make peace.

  16. Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017)

    Film · Theatrical

    After Civil War, 15-year-old Peter Parker desperately wants to be an Avenger. He takes on Adrian Toomes, the Vulture, a salvager selling Chitauri weapons who turns out to be his date Liz's father. Peter turns down Tony's offer to join the team.

  17. Thor: Ragnarok (2017)

    Film · Theatrical

    Odin dies and Hela, his firstborn, conquers Asgard. Thor is stranded on Sakaar, where Hulk is a gladiator. Thor loses an eye and Mjolnir, and Asgard is destroyed in Ragnarok. The refugees' ship meets Thanos's ship.

  18. Black Panther (2018)

    Film · Theatrical

    T'Challa becomes king, then is overthrown by his cousin Killmonger, who wants to arm oppressed people around the world. T'Challa wins back the throne and opens Wakanda up to the world.

  19. Avengers: Infinity War (2018)

    Film · Theatrical

    Thanos collects all six Infinity Stones. Loki, Gamora, Heimdall and Vision die. Thor's axe strikes Thanos but not his head, and he snaps, wiping out half of all life. Spider-Man, Black Panther and most of the Guardians turn to dust.

  20. Ant-Man and the Wasp (2018)

    Film · Theatrical

    Scott, under house arrest, helps Hope and Hank rescue Janet from the Quantum Realm. In the post-credits scene the snap hits, leaving Scott trapped in the Quantum Realm, which later lets him escape into Endgame.

  21. Captain Marvel (2019)

    Film · Theatrical

    In 1995, Kree soldier Vers learns she is really US pilot Carol Danvers. The Kree lied to her, and the Skrulls are refugees. A young Nick Fury loses his eye to Goose the Flerken. Fury names the Avengers Initiative after her call sign.

  22. Avengers: Endgame (2019)

    Film · Theatrical

    Five years after the snap, Scott escapes the Quantum Realm, and the Avengers time-travel to collect the stones. Natasha sacrifices herself on Vormir, Hulk snaps everyone back, and Tony dies snapping Thanos away. Steve returns the stones, lives a life with Peggy, and hands Sam the shield. A 2012 Loki escapes with the Tesseract.

  23. Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019)

    Film · Theatrical

    On a school trip to Europe, Peter inherits Tony's EDITH glasses and is tricked by Mysterio, an illusionist. Mysterio dies, but a doctored video frames Peter, and J. Jonah Jameson reveals that Peter Parker is Spider-Man.

  24. WandaVision (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    A grieving Wanda traps the town of Westview inside sitcom realities with a recreated Vision and twin sons. Agatha Harkness reveals Wanda is the Scarlet Witch. Wanda lets the town go, losing Vision and the boys, and starts reading the Darkhold. S.W.O.R.D. rebuilds a White Vision, who flies off.

  25. The Falcon and the Winter Soldier (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Sam gives up the shield, and the government hands it to John Walker, who murders a Flag Smasher in public. Bucky makes amends and Zemo escapes. Sam takes up the shield as the new Captain America, and Valentina recruits Walker.

  26. Loki (Season 1) (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    The 2012 Loki is arrested by the TVA, which prunes every branch from the Sacred Timeline. He meets variants of himself, including Sylvie, and reaches the Citadel at the End of Time. Sylvie kills He Who Remains, and the multiverse branches out of control.

  27. What If...? (Season 1) (2021)

    Animated · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    The Watcher shows alternate MCU stories: Peggy becomes Captain Carter, T'Challa becomes Star-Lord, Strange loses his heart and his world, and the Avengers fall to zombies. It ends with a team of heroes stopping an Infinity Ultron.

  28. Black Widow (2021)

    Film · Theatrical / Disney+

    Set after Civil War. Natasha reunites with her fake Russian family: sister Yelena, Red Guardian and Melina. She destroys the Red Room and frees the brainwashed Widows. In 2023, Yelena is told Clint is to blame for Natasha's death.

  29. Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings (2021)

    Film · Theatrical

    Shaun, a San Francisco valet, is really Shang-Chi, son of Wenwu, who has led the Ten Rings for 1,000 years. Wenwu is lured by a voice that sounds like his dead wife and dies saving his son from a soul-eating creature. The Ten Rings respond to Wong. Simu Liu is in the announced Doomsday cast.

  30. Eternals (2021)

    Film · Theatrical

    Ten immortals sent by the Celestial Arishem learn that Earth is an egg for a new Celestial, Tiamut, whose birth would destroy all life. They stop it, freezing Tiamut in the Indian Ocean. Introduces Harry Styles as Eros.

  31. Hawkeye (2021)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    At Christmas, Clint teams up with Kate Bishop to deal with his Ronin past. Yelena comes for revenge. Vincent D'Onofrio's Kingpin returns from the Netflix shows as the head of the Tracksuit Mafia, and Echo shoots him.

  32. Spider-Man: No Way Home (2021)

    Film · Theatrical

    With his identity public, Peter asks Strange for a spell, and Peter's meddling breaks it. Villains from other universes arrive: Green Goblin, Doc Ock, Electro, Sandman and Lizard. Aunt May dies. Tobey Maguire's and Andrew Garfield's Spider-Men help cure them. Strange erases Peter Parker from everyone's memory, and Charlie Cox's Matt Murdock appears.

  33. Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness (2022)

    Film · Theatrical

    America Chavez can travel between universes, and Wanda, corrupted by the Darkhold, hunts her to reach her sons in another world. On Earth-838 the Illuminati are slaughtered: Patrick Stewart's Xavier, John Krasinski's Reed Richards, Captain Carter, Black Bolt and Captain Marvel (Maria Rambeau). Wanda destroys the Darkhold and brings Mount Wundagore down on herself. Strange gets a third eye.

  34. Moon Knight (2022)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Gift-shop worker Steven Grant learns he shares a body with mercenary Marc Spector, avatar of the Egyptian moon god Khonshu. They stop Arthur Harrow from freeing Ammit. A third identity, Jake Lockley, is revealed.

  35. Ms. Marvel (2022)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Jersey teen Kamala Khan's bangle unlocks light powers tied to the Noor dimension and her family's history in Partition-era India. Bruno says she has a genetic "mutation", and the X-Men '97 theme plays. In the post-credits scene she swaps places with Captain Marvel.

  36. Thor: Love and Thunder (2022)

    Film · Theatrical

    Gorr the God Butcher wants to kill every god. Jane Foster, dying of cancer, wields a rebuilt Mjolnir as the Mighty Thor and dies in battle. Thor adopts Gorr's daughter, Love.

  37. She-Hulk: Attorney at Law (2022)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Lawyer Jennifer Walters gets Hulk powers from Bruce's blood and runs a superhuman law division. Abomination is paroled, and Bruce returns with his son Skaar. Charlie Cox's Daredevil shows up as both a rival lawyer and a love interest. The finale breaks the fourth wall.

  38. Werewolf by Night (2022)

    Special · Disney+

    A black-and-white horror special. Monster hunters compete for the Bloodstone, and one of them, Jack Russell, turns out to be a werewolf. Man-Thing is his friend. It isn't tied to any other story.

  39. Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022)

    Film · Theatrical

    Wakanda mourns T'Challa. Namor's undersea Talokan threatens war over vibranium, and young inventor Riri Williams is caught in the middle. Ramonda dies, and Shuri becomes Black Panther. T'Challa's son is revealed in Haiti.

  40. I Am Groot (2022)

    Animated · Disney+ · 2 seasons, 10 episodes

    Short animated pieces about Baby Groot's mischief on the Guardians' ship. Two seasons (2022, 2023).

  41. The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special (2022)

    Special · Disney+

    Drax and Mantis kidnap Kevin Bacon as a Christmas present for Peter. It reveals that Mantis is Peter's half-sister, and the team now owns Knowhere.

  42. Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania (2023)

    Film · Theatrical

    Cassie's signal pulls the family into the Quantum Realm, where Janet's exiled ally Kang the Conqueror rules. Kang is seemingly defeated, but a council of his variants gathers. Marvel later dropped the Kang story, and Doctor Doom became the saga's villain instead.

  43. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 (2023)

    Film · Theatrical

    Rocket is badly wounded, and the team raids the High Evolutionary, the scientist who mutilated him. Rocket's past is shown and he becomes captain. The team goes separate ways, and Peter goes home to his grandfather on Earth.

  44. Secret Invasion (2023)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Rogue Skrulls led by Gravik have replaced world leaders and try to spark a world war. Maria Hill and Talos die, Rhodey is revealed to have been a Skrull for years, and G'iah gains the powers of every Avenger.

  45. Loki (Season 2) (2023)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    The TVA's loom is failing under the branching timelines. Loki learns to time-slip, meets Victor Timely and keeps reliving the collapse. He finally takes the throne at the End of Time and holds the branches together as a living Yggdrasil. Tom Hiddleston is in the announced Doomsday cast.

  46. What If...? (Season 2) (2023)

    Animated · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Nine more alternate tales, including Nebula as a noir detective, 80s Avengers, and the introduction of Kahhori. Captain Carter returns, and the Watcher's ally Strange Supreme imprisons rogue variants.

  47. The Marvels (2023)

    Film · Theatrical

    Carol, Monica Rambeau and Kamala swap places whenever they use their powers. Kree leader Dar-Benn tears holes in space. Monica is trapped in another universe where her mother is Binary, and she meets Kelsey Grammer's Beast. Kamala starts recruiting Kate Bishop for a Young Avengers team.

  48. Echo (2024)

    Series · Disney+ / Hulu · 5 episodes

    After shooting Kingpin in Hawkeye, Maya Lopez goes home to Oklahoma and connects with her Choctaw ancestors' powers. Kingpin survives and comes after her. She uses her powers to heal his trauma, and Fisk heads back to New York to run for mayor. It opens with a Daredevil fight.

  49. Deadpool & Wolverine (2024)

    Film · Theatrical

    The TVA plans to let Deadpool's world (Earth-10005) die slowly, because its anchor being, Logan, died. Wade grabs a "worst" Wolverine variant. They're dumped in the Void, ruled by Cassandra Nova, where they find Blade, Elektra, X-23, Gambit and Johnny Storm. They save the timeline, and this Logan stays on Earth-10005.

  50. Agatha All Along (2024)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Freed from Wanda's spell, Agatha leads a coven down the Witches' Road. Teen is revealed as Billy Maximoff reborn, and Rio Vidal is Death herself. Agatha dies and returns as a ghost guiding Billy, who sets off to find his brother Tommy.

  51. What If...? (Season 3) (2024)

    Animated · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    The final season: Hulk vs. kaiju, the Red Guardian and Winter Soldier in the 80s, and a war in which the Watcher himself is hunted.

  52. Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man (2025)

    Animated · Disney+ · 10 episodes

    An alternate-universe Peter Parker's first year, in a comic-book art style. Norman Osborn becomes his mentor instead of Tony Stark, and Doc Ock is introduced. A second season is coming in late 2026.

  53. Captain America: Brave New World (2025)

    Film · Theatrical

    Thaddeus Ross is now President, and adamantium has been found on Celestial Island. Samuel Sterns, the Leader, manipulates events through mind control. Sam takes on the role with Joaquin Torres as his Falcon, and Ross turns into the Red Hulk at the White House. Sam starts reassembling the Avengers.

  54. Daredevil: Born Again (2025)

    Series · Disney+ · 9 episodes

    Bullseye kills Foggy, and Matt Murdock hangs up the suit. Wilson Fisk is elected mayor of New York and creates an anti-vigilante task force. Frank Castle is back, the serial killer Muse is stopped, and Fisk declares martial law. Matt builds an army to take the city back.

  55. Thunderbolts* (2025)

    Film · Theatrical

    Valentina tries to wipe out her own expendable agents, and Yelena, Ghost, John Walker and Taskmaster survive. With Bucky and Red Guardian they face Bob, a super-soldier who is also the Void, a dark force that swallows New York. Yelena reaches Bob, and Valentina announces them as the "New Avengers". The team is in the Doomsday cast.

  56. Ironheart (2025)

    Series · Disney+ · 6 episodes

    Kicked out of MIT, Riri Williams goes back to Chicago and joins the Hood's crew to fund her armour. Her AI is a recreation of her dead friend Natalie. Hood's powers come from a demonic cloak, and the season ends with Riri making a deal with Mephisto.

  57. The Fantastic Four: First Steps (2025)

    Film · Theatrical

    On the retro-futurist Earth-828 the Fantastic Four are the world's only heroes. The Silver Surfer heralds Galactus, who wants Sue's unborn son Franklin. Sue gives birth, and the team moves the whole planet's population to beat Galactus. Franklin restores Sue's life. In the post-credits scene, Doom visits Franklin.

  58. Eyes of Wakanda (2025)

    Animated · Disney+ · 4 episodes

    Four animated episodes across history about Wakanda's Hatut Zeraze war dogs, who recover stolen vibranium. The story runs from the Bronze Age to the Trojan War, and one episode features an early Iron Fist.

  59. Marvel Zombies (2025)

    Animated · Disney+ · 4 episodes

    An adult-rated animated miniseries. In the What If zombie universe, Kamala, Kate Bishop, Yelena and others fight a Queen of the Dead version of Wanda.

  60. Wonder Man (2026)

    Series · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    Struggling actor Simon Williams, who secretly has powers, auditions for a reboot of the film Wonder Man while hiding his abilities from Hollywood's anti-super rules. Ben Kingsley's Trevor Slattery returns as his acting partner. It was released in January 2026.

  61. Daredevil: Born Again (Season 2) (2026)

    Series · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    Matt's resistance takes on Mayor Fisk's regime. In the finale, Karen Page is on trial and Matt cross-examines Fisk, then publicly reveals he is Daredevil. Karen is cleared and Fisk is pushed into a deal, but Matt is arrested. Jessica Jones returns and ends the season on a cliffhanger.

  62. The Punisher: One Last Kill (2026)

    Special · Disney+

    A roughly 45-minute, mature-rated Marvel Television Special co-written by Jon Bernthal and director Reinaldo Marcus Green. It premiered 12 May 2026, the same week as Born Again Season 2's finale, and carries Frank Castle into Spider-Man: Brand New Day.

  63. Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026)

    Film · Theatrical

    Nobody remembers Peter Parker, so he is Spider-Man full time, but alone. His powers start changing in ways he can't control. A body-jumping psychic threat targets Damage Control, and it's tied to Sadie Sink's Jean Grey searching for her sister. Damage Control's Bill Metzger is the real villain. Scorpion, Tombstone and the Punisher appear. It opened 31 July 2026 with a record-breaking weekend.

  64. VisionQuest (2026)

    Series · Disney+ · 8 episodes

    The final part of the WandaVision trilogy. The rebuilt White Vision tries to recover his memories and his humanity. James Spader's Ultron, Faran Tahir's Raza (from Iron Man) and a human E.D.I.T.H. appear. It premiered 14 October 2026.

  65. Avengers: Doomsday (2026)

    Film · Theatrical

    In cinemas 18 December 2026. Robert Downey Jr. plays Doctor Doom, and the trailer shows an army of Sentinels. The confirmed cast brings together MCU Avengers (including Chris Evans' Steve Rogers), Wakandans, the Thunderbolts and the Fantastic Four, plus Fox X-Men veterans: Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, Alan Cumming, Kelsey Grammer and Channing Tatum's Gambit.

  66. Avengers: Secret Wars (2027)

    Film · Theatrical

    Planned for December 2027 as the end of the Multiverse Saga. Sadie Sink's Jean Grey is confirmed to return. Plot details are not public yet.
