Letters page

About

A fan-made guide to how Marvel's films and series fit together across studios, streaming services and universes.

What's covered

Marvel Studios' films and Disney+ series, Fox's X-Men and Fantastic Four films, Sony's Spider-Man and Venom films, the Netflix Defenders shows, Marvel Television's ABC, Hulu and Freeform series, the 2000s films from New Line, Universal and Lionsgate, and the main animated series and films.

How connections are decided

A line between two titles means one of three things: they share a story (a sequel, a crossover or a returning character), the title is a major release that others refer back to, or it feeds directly into Avengers: Doomsday and Secret Wars. If none of these apply, the title is still listed but marked Standalone, and no line is drawn just to fill space.

Story years are only given where the film makes them clear. Some placements, such as Logan, are debated by fans, and those entries say so.

Keeping it current

Data is current to October 2026. Upcoming releases are tagged Upcoming, and cast details only include what has been officially announced. Rumours aren't included.
